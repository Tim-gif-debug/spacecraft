Privacy Policy – Orion’s Space Adventure

Orion’s Space Adventure is published by Comic Book Historians LLC.

This app does not collect, store or transmit any personal data.

  • No account is required.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking code is included.
  • All art, music and sound is inside the app, and the game works offline.
  • Game Center is optional. If the player is signed in to Game Center on the device, the app sends two leaderboard scores (Krystal Rush distance and total stars earned) to Apple’s Game Center. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple’s own privacy policy. The developer receives no personal data from it.
  • There are no in-app purchases. Stars, upgrades and styles are earned by playing and have no cash value.
  • Progress, scores and settings are stored only on the device. Deleting the app deletes them, and so does RESET PROGRESS in the game’s Settings.
  • The app does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts or any other device sensor.
