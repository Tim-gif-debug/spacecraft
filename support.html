Orion’s Space Adventure

An action adventure with 29 story missions across six chapters, plus a harder Admiral Mode. Each mission plays differently: Orion catches crops at the Cyber Farm, rewires a power substation, lands a falling taxi, taps out Morse code to a friend, wins over a reluctant army leader, rallies the troops to a beat and takes on Spacecraft Nova. Every character and name in the app is original.

Based on the short story “The Fascinating Spacecraft” by Maximilian Grand.

Support

Questions or problems: Comicbookhistorians@gmail.com

How to play

Hold the device sideways. Each chapter shows its controls at the start. KICK three times for a combo, DASH to pass through danger, and watch the KRYSTAL meter, which powers Orion’s abilities. Stars earned in each chapter buy upgrades under GEAR.

Settings

SETTINGS on the title screen has switches for music, sound, vibration, glow effects, an easy mode and left-handed buttons. RESET PROGRESS erases the saved game.

Privacy

The app collects no personal data. It goes online only to send Game Center scores, and only when the device is signed in to Game Center. Progress is saved on the device only. The full policy is on the privacy page.
